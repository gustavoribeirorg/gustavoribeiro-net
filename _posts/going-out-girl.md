title: Going out with a girl from Tinder
categories: [Cotidiano]
tags: [cotidiano, pessoal]

I would start this blog entry by saying, \"Today was a good day,\" but
it actually happened last Saturday. I\'m still dealing with my abrupt
breakup. I can\'t say anymore that I didn\'t see it coming because
almost two months later, I can think about a lot of signs that we showed
each other.

<!--more-->

However, I\'m not writing about that. As mentioned, Saturday was a good
day. I connected with this girl on Tinder, and we chatted for about a
week before arranging our in-person meeting. I suggested visiting a
newly opened brewery pub in my city.

It was my second time there, but the first one with good company. That
girl works in social media, but she\'s also really talented at writing
poems. She talked a lot about her passion, read some poems to me, told
stories, and we laughed a lot.

It was a cold night, and we were not dressed for it, so I invited her to
take a ride around the city in my car. And there we were. She didn\'t
stop talking, and I think this happens when people feel comfortable with
the company.

We went to a quiet neighborhood, but she thought it was too calm, so I
took her to a skatepark. I grabbed a blanket that I had in the car and
offered it to her, and we sat by the ramp and talked about the graffiti,
an owl, and many other things.

She wanted more beer, so we went to a gas station and stayed there for
an hour or more. While we were there, the thought passed through my mind
several times. It felt like we had already known each other for a long
time.

The night wasn\'t over yet, and I took her to a different place. We went
to a park that people call \"Praça da Rodonaves.\" Rodonaves is a
logistics company, and they maintain the park by trimming the plants and
trees in the shape of flying saucers (\"naves\" means \"ships\" in
Portuguese).

It was almost 4 a.m. when she decided that she wanted to go home. I
dropped her off at her place and then returned to my apartment. It was
actually a good night. Despite everything I\'ve been through, I felt
well again.
