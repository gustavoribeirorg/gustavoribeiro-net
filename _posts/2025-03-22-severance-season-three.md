## James Eagan will try to adopt Helly R.

As we saw in episode 9, Eagan came to the MDR room and found Helly R.
alone with the lights off, trying to memorize the directions left by
Irving B. to the dark hallway with the elevator at the end. After saying
that she tricked him, James called her \"my Helly.\" In the next
episode, they continue the dialogue.

<!--more-->

Eagan goes on saying that he doesn\'t love his daughter because he
doesn\'t see Kier in her anymore after she grew up, stating that he
sired others in the shadows but couldn\'t see Kier in them either, until
finally saying that he saw Kier again in Helly R. She treats him with a
pen, and James proceeds by saying, \"There he is.\"

Maybe Helly R. will play along with this to survive and will try to add
a condition to save Mark S. too. Mark S. had sex with Helly R. and
Helena Eagan. Probably, she will be pregnant in season 3 and will use
this against James Eagan to get what she wants, even if it\'s a fake
pregnancy, saying that Mark S. is the father of an Eagan.

## Gemma and Devon will try to save Mark Scout

Gemma, a.k.a. Ms. Casey, will join forces with her sister-in-law to try
to save Mark Scout. They already know they can\'t count on Mark S., so
they will elaborate a plot to bring Mark S. out of the Lumon building. I
don\'t think that this will be an easy task because Mark S. now knows
that if he gets out of the severed floor, he might not get back.

To accomplish this, both will count on help from Helena Eagan and Ms.
Selvig, a.k.a. Ms. Harmony Cobel. Helena will be driven by hatred after
discovering his plans with Helly R. On the other hand, Cobel wants to
get credit for her invention, the severance procedure.

## Reintegration will be a central point

For James\'s plan to work, Helly R. and Mark S. need to be permanently
severed, but Mark S./Scout\'s reintegration will start working, and both
personalities will fight each other for control. Gemma knows that she is
severed and will try to communicate with Ms. Casey to know more about
the severed floor, maybe even trying to go down there.

Helena knows more about Lumon and the severance procedure, and with the
help of Selvig/Cobel, will try to do something to take over Helly R.
Maybe a reintegration when she is out of the Lumon building. I think
Seth Milchick will change sides too, but because he wants more
recognition, and this desire will clash with Selvig/Cobel\'s plans.
However, he will be part of the strategy to get Mark S. and Helly out of
the building.

Irving will be back.
