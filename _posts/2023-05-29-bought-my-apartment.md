Next month will be the first month that my mom and I will not pay the
rent. Since she came to Ribeirão Preto, she has lived in many different
houses and apartment buildings. I was born in one house, raised in
another, and spent my teenage years in three others.

<!--more-->

My brother hasn\'t lived with my mom for almost ten years because she
shared the house with two other girls. We didn\'t have a place to call
home, and I\'ve never felt connected to any place where I lived with my
family.

Anytime the landlord would call and tell us that we needed to move. The
first house that I remember had three rooms, and my brother, my mom, and
I slept in the same bedroom for more than 10 years.

When I was 15 years old, we moved to a home with three bedrooms, and
that was the first time that I slept alone. It was a nice apartment, and
we lived in that apartment complex for 7 or more years.

About a year ago, I started living with my girlfriend, but the dream of
having my own place was still with me. I wanted to buy a house so my mom
would stop paying rent and I could have a place to call my own.

My girlfriend at the time had bought an apartment, and I followed the
process with her. I also tried to buy one at the time, but with no
success. Almost one year later, I talked to a realtor and tried again.
This time, I wanted to buy the apartment where my mom already lived.

It was too expensive, but the realtor found another one in the same
apartment complex for almost half the price. It needed a new paint job
and a lot of other things that needed fixing, but we finally have our
own apartment now.

The plan was for my mother to live there alone while I was with my
girlfriend, but destiny had something different in mind, and now my
mother and I are living together again. I\'m really happy and have a lot
of plans for the apartment.

For the first two weeks after I got the keys, we cleaned and repainted
everything. It\'s an old apartment, and it was empty for a long time, so
it was really dirty. A friend of my mom came over and helped with the
cleaning, and we hired a painter for the walls, windows, doors, and
closets.

I tried to wash the curtains in the washing machine, but they came out
all crimped. Luckily, my washing machine wasn\'t big enough to fit more
than one at a time. After that failed attempt, I cleaned the remaining
curtains with a cloth. They still look a little grimy, but I managed to
remove the more visible dirt and dust.

My bedroom still needs some work. My desk doesn\'t fit the way I wanted
it to, and I will ask a friend to help me saw it. Besides that,
everything is in its place. We even have a hammock in the living room!
It\'s a perfect place to read or watch TV.

It is a great achievement for my family and me. Soon my brother will be
with us too and enjoy our family home. We only have two bedrooms, but
we\'ll find a way for him to sleep. He\'ll stay only for a few weeks,
but I\'m looking forward to being with him and enjoying our home
together!
