title: Trip to São Thomé das Letras
categories: [Cotidiano]

---

This was my first long trip driving a car and I was really anxious about
that. Me and my girlfriend planned our trip with months in advance so
nothing could go wrong. I think that we wanted to go to São Thomé das
Letras since we first met.

<!--more-->

It was Friday and I still had some stuffs to do from work, so my girl
took care of most of the preparation for the trip while I was finishing
my job. After some delay, mostly because of me, we were on the road.

The car, a Volkswagen Gol 1995, was full of bags, food and joy. My girl
started driving and we would change sits after she gets tired. Five
hours of driving was expecting us. The first two were fun. We listened
to pop music and talked about the landscape and things that we saw on
the road.

But the night came with rain. We exchanged sits and I was behind the
wheel. I barely could see the road. Minas Gerais roadway is known for
the precarious conditions, holes, and big trucks. Most are single lane
too. So, I drove slowly.

The cars that came against me with high beams obfuscated my view and I
couldn't see nothing for 2 or 3 seconds. It was scary. At some point, I
saw a car behind me and I let It pass. My plan was to follow its
backlight because I was afraid of getting out of the road.

I could see where I was going, but not the holes, and the car passed
through a lot of them. My girlfriend was mad at me because if I wasn't
late, we wouldn't take the highway at night. When we finally found our
AirBnB, we found relief too.

We woke up next day and saw how beautiful our guesthouse was. It was in
the middle of nature, and I could hear the birds singing, the sound of
running water and we were visited a lot of times by two cute dogs.

São Thome das Letras is a small city, but it's full of tourists all the
time, even late night. Me and my girlfriend went to a lot of places, ate
things that we didn't know it existed, like "peixinho", a delicious
fried leaf, and of course we experimented cheeses and coffees.

But we went there for the natural beauties. We went to different tourist
spots, like Casa da Pirâmide, Morro do Cruzeiro and others places, and
we fell in love for the waterfalls. We passed a lot of times talking and
chilling by the waterfalls.

We stayed for 5 days and, this time, we left in the morning. We took the
opportunity to pass by Três Corações and I visited the house where Pelé
was born. It was a little house with some memorabilia. Was a nice memory
for the king of football, but São Thomé was way more than I could
expect.
