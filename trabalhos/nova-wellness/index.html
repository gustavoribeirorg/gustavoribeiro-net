Before Getting Started

What happens during my consultation?

We'll learn about your health history, discuss your goals, and determine whether a personalized medical weight loss program is right for you.

Is every treatment plan the same?

No. Every plan is built around your body, your health history, and your goals — two patients rarely follow the same protocol.

Is GLP-1 therapy right for everyone?

Not necessarily. GLP-1 therapy is one of several options our medical team may recommend after reviewing your health history during your consultation.

Will I receive ongoing support?

Yes. Your journey includes continued medical guidance and support so you can build habits that last well beyond the first few months.

How much does treatment cost?

Pricing depends on the plan our medical team recommends for you. We'll walk you through all costs transparently during your consultation — no surprises.

How soon can I get started?

Many patients begin their program shortly after their consultation. Book your visit and we'll map out your next steps.
